## UX and verification

The early preview jump was an explicit 1.5 s pre-roll shared by both listening buttons. **Écouter** now starts at the selected cut and stops at its end. **Raccord** retains the pre-roll and skips the selected passage to audition the join, including unchecked proposals. Help text and tooltips explain the difference. Mouse-wheel zoom is anchored to the pointer and synchronized with the slider, including after horizontal scrolling.

Validation: 125 backend tests across Facecam, cut recovery, cut editing and protected silence editing; eight browser tests with real video playback; production frontend build. Recovery tests verify original-clock mapping, separate cache reuse/invalidation, unchanged source assets, real-pause splitting, unchecked recovered proposals and failure handling. Real-audio evaluation used the existing large-v3 recognizer and French word alignment on CPU; the actual project analysis was refreshed through its API.

Local evidence is under ignored `backend/data/research/facecam-cuts-5058388c9ebd/`; production recovery and analysis caches live beside the original recording. This is a targeted recovery for stretched-word failures, not a guarantee that every false start will be transcribed or proposed.
